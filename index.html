AI changed the work. The apprenticeship model has not caught up.

Rogo Academy rebuilds, inside the workflow, the learning that repetition used to provide. It has two parts: how to work with AI to a professional standard, and the technical fundamentals every analyst should own without it.

Two parts, one standard

Work faster with AI, and still be able to explain every number.

Finished output is becoming a less reliable signal of understanding. The answer is not to bring back inefficient work. It is to teach review and fundamentals deliberately, alongside the new way of working.

Part 1 · AI-Assisted Work

Quality & usage

Stop the slop: a working standard, the failure modes to catch and a QC pass to run before anything reaches a partner or investment committee.

Use it where it compounds: sourcing screens, market maps, meeting prep, first-draft memos and portfolio updates, each with a prompt and a list of what to verify.

Part 2 · Technical Fundamentals

Retention you can test

Short teaching, interactive labs and practice checks on the concepts behind every investment decision: accounting, unit economics, VC math, valuation, terms, diligence and fund economics.

Each module ends with a graded case in Felix: a fresh case, the answer withheld, your own work graded and a retest with new numbers.

How it works

Learn, practice, prove, retest.

Felix works as a tutor, not an executor. It sets the work, withholds the answer and grades what you built.

01

Learn

One concept at a time, taught on a single fictional company so the numbers connect across modules.

02

Practice

Change inputs in the labs and watch the outputs move. Practice checks explain every answer once you commit.

03

Prove

Run the graded case in Felix. You get a fresh case, no answer key and a rubric score on your own work, then you explain it back.

04

Retest

New numbers and a new structure, about a month later. The test is whether you hold the score, not whether you passed once.

Principles

What the Academy will and will not do.

01

You own the answer

AI can help. If your name is on the work, you can explain every assumption without asking AI again.

02

Teaching, not answers

Graded cases never show the solution before you submit. Hints come one at a time.

03

Private to the learner

Progress stays in your browser. The pilot has no manager view of individual results.

04

Built for finance by finance

Written and reviewed by former bankers and investors, on cases that look like the real job.
